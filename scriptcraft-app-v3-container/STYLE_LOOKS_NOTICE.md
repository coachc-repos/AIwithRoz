# Style looks

Whiteboard, chalkboard, sketchnote, and marker on paper follow the published
descriptions in Lemo-Opuscar (https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar).

That repository's LICENSE is mixed. It is not a single MIT or Apache grant.

- Code, including `styles/whiteboard/demo/engine/wb.js`, is MIT.
- The style documents (`STYLE.md`, `DEMO.md`, and the other Markdown) are
  CC BY-NC 4.0. Putting those documents, whole or adapted, into a product
  needs a separate commercial licence from Lemomo (LemoLab).
- Demo films, some narration, fonts, and third-party marks are excluded
  or stay under their own terms.

Because that licence is restrictive for this product, these looks do not
copy Lemo-Opuscar code, fonts, films, or other assets. The drawing code in
`static/explainer/board.js` and `explainer_styles.py` is original. It follows
only the published descriptions: materials, colours, a travelling camera,
stroke-by-stroke writing, and what each look is not.

Board lettering uses Kalam Bold by the Indian Type Foundry, under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1. The file is `static/explainer/fonts/Kalam-Bold.ttf`
(a basic-Latin subset). It is not part of Lemo-Opuscar.
